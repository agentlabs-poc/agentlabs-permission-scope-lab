# Team grant changes — Q-097

Agreed for this discussion; recorded in the scratchpad only. The handbook
repository remains frozen. Tenant is implied.

## Rule

Validate the whole proposed resulting authority against the acting administrator's
current valid supporting authority, not just the permissions or scope being added.
Separate authorization to perform the grant/assignment change is also required.
Parent-team and parent-grant ceilings and other mandatory restrictions still apply.

## Om and Maya example

This is a hypothetical change scenario, not an update to the existing Team1
grant files. Assume both administrators have the required change authority.

| State or action | Outcome |
|---|---|
| Maya's supporting authority allows FIN read | Her assignment ceiling is FIN read. |
| Om validly expands Team1 to FIN read/write | Team1 may have more authority than Maya; this alone is not a breach of her boundary. |
| Maya proposes a change retaining write | Rejected: the whole resulting authority exceeds her ceiling. |
| Maya proposes replacing it with FIN read | May pass, provided she is authorized to make that change and all other constraints hold. |

The rule also applies to scope expansion: Maya cannot retain a wider boundary
that her valid supporting authority does not cover.

## Rationale and consequences

- This makes each administrator responsible for the complete authority they
  establish, not merely their edit's added permissions or constraints.
- Maya can propose one complete valid result. She need not first narrow and then
  add through separate operations; this does not finalize transaction mechanics.
- Narrowing may withdraw legitimate access Om supplied to other users. It must
  be an explicit authorized change, not automatic cleanup. Being able to propose
  less authority does not itself grant permission to replace or delete records.
- The trade-off is deliberate: Maya cannot preserve authority outside her ceiling
  while making an otherwise small change to that same grant.
- If Maya receives Team1's expanded business authority through valid membership,
  her effective ceiling may expand too. Ownership alone does not grant that access.
- Om must independently have valid source authority and administrative authority
  for his expansion; being an owner is not an exception to either requirement.
- Changes must follow explicit revision/adoption rules. No silent widening of
  shared definitions, other assignments, or dependent child authority is approved.
