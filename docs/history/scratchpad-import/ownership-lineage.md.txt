# Owner changes and team-held lineage — Q-099

Discussion explanation, not a performed ownership change. The repository remains
frozen. SVGs are preferred where they materially clarify relationships or flow.

## Expected behavior under team-held support

An explicit authorized ownership transfer changes who receives the owner/admin
grant. It does not automatically change the team's business grants, their
assignments, selected revisions, parent links, or member relationships.
With those dependencies unchanged, downstream authority remains unchanged.

For the current example, Team1's FIN assignment continues to support Team2's
FIN-read authority, and Nutan continues to receive that authority through her
Team2 membership. The diagram illustrates a potential owner-list change; Om,
Maya, Suma, and Nutan's stored scratchpad records have not been changed.

## Necessary distinctions

- This assumes the continuing source is team-held. A route that explicitly
  depends on a departing owner's personal assignment or membership cannot
  silently be treated as team-held. Q-096's refinement of Q-093 still requires
  a clear dependency contract; no automatic rebinding is implied.
- Removing an owner removes that owner-list route, not every other grant the
  person holds. Maya's older direct administrative grants still exist in the
  scratchpad and are not revoked by an owner-list edit.
- Changing owners must not import their personal scopes or permissions into
  the team's authority. Changing the actual authority is a separate authorized
  operation, subject to the agreed parent and complete-result bounds.
- If the actual supporting team assignment is removed, expires, or otherwise
  becomes unusable, its dependent route still stops. Ownership continuity does
  not override missing or invalid authority support.
- Current owner grants contain `auth:group::write`, not grant-assignment or a
  finalized ownership-transfer permission. This illustration does not establish
  which actor is authorized to perform a transfer.

Rationale: keep administrative continuity separate from the source of recipients'
authority. Stable team-held support avoids making unrelated owner rotation an
implicit rewrite of the authority lineage.
